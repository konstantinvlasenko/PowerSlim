Demonstrate the different time attributes that can be evaluated
''On Windows a 1 second sleep can measure slightly under 1s (timer granularity), so the lower bounds allow 10%''

| Start-Sleep -Seconds $arg1 |
| arg1 | Time.Seconds? | Time.Milliseconds? | Time.Days? | Time.Hours? | Time.Minutes? |
|  1   |     > 0.9     |        > 900       |    < 1     |    < 1      |     < 1       |
