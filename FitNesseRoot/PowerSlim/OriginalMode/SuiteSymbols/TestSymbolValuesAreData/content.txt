''A symbol is also a !-PowerShell-! variable. Its value is never pasted into the code: quotes, $ and ; in it stay text''
!|script|
|$Quoted=|eval|"it's a ""quote"" and `$notAVariable; exit"|
|check|eval|$Quoted|it's a "quote" and $notAVariable; exit|
|check|eval|"[$Quoted]"|[it's a "quote" and $notAVariable; exit]|
|check|eval|'[$Quoted]'|[it's a "quote" and $notAVariable; exit]|
|check|eval|$Quoted.Length|38|

''Symbols keep their type''
!|script|
|$Number=|eval|41|
|check|eval|$Number + 1|42|
|$List=|eval|@(1, 2, 3)|
|check|eval|$List.Count|3|
